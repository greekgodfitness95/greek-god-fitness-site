Personal Training in Limassol

Your questions, answered. Private, one-to-one coaching in Germasogeia, Limassol.

Who is my personal trainer?

Oliver Elia, founder of Greek God Fitness. Oliver studied personal training for four years in Greater Manchester, UK, then spent four years as a personal trainer for Royal Caribbean on the world's biggest cruise ships, Harmony, Symphony and Oasis of the Seas, before bringing that experience home to Limassol.

What can personal training help me with?

Every program is built around your goals. Oliver specialises in weight loss, muscle gain, posture alignment and detox, and in building overall fitness, strength and wellness that lasts. Pair your training with a nutrition plan from The Diet Clinic for the best results.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first personal training session is free and comes with no commitment. We use it to get to know you, see your strengths and the areas to improve, and then design a program exclusively for you.

Where do you train?

At the Greek God Fitness studio on Spyrou Kyprianou, Germasogeia 4043, in Limassol, Cyprus, a short drive from anywhere in Limassol.

Is it right for beginners?

Absolutely. Whether you are starting your fitness journey or you are an experienced athlete, every session is tailored to your level, so you progress safely and enjoy every workout.

How do I book?

Message Oliver on WhatsApp. We usually reply within 30 minutes, and together we'll choose a day and time for your free first session.
