Meet the Coach

Oliver Elia

Welcome to the heart of Oliver's world! Get to know the person behind the passion, the vision, and the dedication. This is more than just a bio; it's a glimpse into the story that brought me here.

I studied in the UK, Greater Manchester, for my personal training qualification for 4 years. Then I came back to Cyprus where I tried to start my own personal training but struggled and understood it's not that easy but that never stopped me.

I eventually found my way to working on cruise ships as a Personal Trainer for Royal Caribbean on the world's biggest ships, Harmony of the Seas, Symphony of the Seas and Oasis of the Seas. After being in the Caribbean for 4 years I learnt and grew a lot, specializing in detox, weight loss, muscle gain and posture alignment.

Once back in Cyprus I had more knowledge, more experience and my determination was unstoppable. I started Greek God Fitness with one goal — to help people achieve their goals, and I haven't looked back since.

The reason I got into personal training and the fitness industry is that I've always wanted to help people and being a part of someone's fitness journey is so rewarding.

"It would be a shame to grow old and never know your body's full potential."

Thank you for taking the time to get to know me better. I'm excited to share this journey with you and look forward to meeting you and starting your journey to a healthier you together.
